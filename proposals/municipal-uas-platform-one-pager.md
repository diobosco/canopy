# Municipal UAS Platform — Capability One-Pager

**One shared surveillance-drone system, tasked on demand by every municipal department.**
Faster emergency response · lower-risk inspections · richer planning data · shared cost, high utilization.

---

## Why One Shared System

A single airframe fleet, interchangeable sensor payloads, command software, trained crews, and a
governance framework — re-tasked across departments. Utilization is spread, so **cost per flight
hour falls and return on the capital asset rises**. One capital purchase, allocated by usage.

## The Value Levers

- **Cost avoidance** — replaces helicopter hours, scaffolding/cherry-pickers, road closures, survey contractors.
- **Faster response, lower risk** — minutes saved save lives; crews kept out of hazard.
- **Recovery & revenue** — faster road clearance, stronger disaster-claim documentation, enforcement recovery.
- **Compounding data asset** — recurring imagery feeds GIS and digital-twin programmes.

---

## Governed for Public Trust

Published use policy · public flight dashboard · data-retention limits · tamper-evident audit logs ·
geofencing · privacy-impact review · BVLOS/Remote-ID compliance · certified pilots.
**Clear exclusions:** no weaponized payloads, no chemical agents against people; crowd use is
observational public-safety monitoring only.

---

## Pricing (Formula-Based, Auditable)

Recover Total Cost of Ownership + margin; justify on value/cost-avoidance. Choose **per-flight-hour**,
**per-mission**, **subscription (Drone-as-a-Service)**, or **tiered**. Department cost allocated by
share of usage. Full formula framework available.
