# Municipal UAS Platform
## One Shared Drone System. Every Department. Faster, Safer, Cheaper.

A pitch deck for local government decision-makers (council · procurement · public-safety leadership)

---

# The Problem

- Rising demand for **faster emergency response** and **better situational awareness**
- Infrastructure inspection is **slow, costly, and dangerous** (helicopters, scaffolding, road closures)
- Planning and environmental teams lack **current aerial data**
- Every department buying its own tools = **duplicated cost, low utilization**

> Departments need an aerial capability. Buying it ten times over is the wrong answer.

---

# The Idea: One System, Many Missions

- **One** airframe fleet + interchangeable sensor payloads
- **One** command software, crew, and governance framework
- **Tasked on demand** by police, fire, emergency mgmt, public works, traffic, planning, environment
- Utilization spread across departments → **cost per flight hour falls, ROI rises**

---

# What It Does — Public Safety

- **Drone-as-First-Responder:** on scene before patrol cars; live video for commanders
- **Search & Rescue:** thermal imaging for missing persons, water and collapse rescues
- **Fire:** wildfire mapping, hotspots, overhead command view
- **Events security:** crowd density, bottlenecks, lost persons, perimeter overwatch
- **Medical delivery:** AEDs, naloxone, life vests to cut-off victims

---

# What It Does — Civic Operations

- **Traffic:** congestion monitoring, incident detection, rapid collision-scene mapping
- **Public works:** bridges, roofs, dams, utilities, drainage — inspected without scaffolding
- **Planning & GIS:** orthomaps, 3D city models, change detection — a living data asset
- **Environment:** illegal dumping, water/air quality, code compliance
- **Critical infrastructure:** perimeter security, intrusion verification

---

# The Business Case

- **Cost avoidance:** replaces helicopter hours, cherry-pickers, road closures, survey contractors
- **Faster response, lower risk:** minutes saved save lives; crews kept out of hazard
- **Recovery & revenue:** faster road clearance, stronger disaster-claim documentation
- **Compounding data asset:** imagery feeds GIS and digital-twin programmes
- **Shared cost model:** one capital purchase, allocated by usage across departments

---

# Built for Public Trust

- Published, public-facing use policy and flight dashboard
- Strict **data-retention limits** and tamper-evident audit logs
- **Geofencing**, privacy-impact review, purpose limitation
- BVLOS waivers, Remote-ID, certified pilots, airspace coordination
- **Clear exclusions:** no weaponized payloads; no chemical agents against people; crowd use is observation only

> Transparency is not overhead — it is what makes the programme procurable and politically durable.

---

# How We Price It

- Recover **Total Cost of Ownership** + margin; justify on **value & cost-avoidance**
- Flexible models: **per-flight-hour**, **per-mission**, **subscription (Drone-as-a-Service)**, **tiered**
- Department cost allocation by share of usage
- Full formula framework provided — auditable, no black boxes

---

# Proposed Path

1. **Discovery workshop** — prioritize mission set with stakeholder departments
2. **Governance & privacy framework** — drafted with legal and community input
3. **Pilot** — 2–3 high-value, low-controversy use cases
4. **ROI review** — measured against the value model
5. **Scale-out** — across departments on the shared-platform model
